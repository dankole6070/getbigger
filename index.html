THE 12 WEEK COURSE

A structured 12-week plan to help you build strength, muscle and confidence.

WEEKS 1–2

Learn the fundamentals. Master your technique, establish your routine and build good training habits.

WEEKS 3–4

Start progressing. Increase training intensity and begin tracking your strength and performance.

WEEKS 5–6

Build momentum. Progressive overload becomes a major focus as you continue developing your physique.

WEEKS 7–8

Push your limits. Training becomes more challenging while maintaining proper technique and recovery.

WEEKS 9–10

Get stronger. Fine-tune your training and focus on consistent progress week after week.

WEEKS 11–12

Finish strong. Review your progress, measure your results and build the foundation for your next goal.
